This project evaluates a minimal Deception-in-Depth Honeynet
architecture composed of network-level honeypots, host-level Active
Directory deception, and data-level cloud decoy assets. The study
experimentally measures attacker path deviation (misdirection), delay,
and engagement across layered deception components, with observed
actions correlated to high-level MITRE ATT&CK tactics. The design
prioritizes experimental clarity and reproducibility over operational
scale. Informed by recent research on multilayer cyber deception
architectures (<https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16430v1>), the work provides
empirical insights into the behavioural impact and trade-offs of layered
deception mechanisms.
